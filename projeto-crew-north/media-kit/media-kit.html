Who we are

Built by a courier,
for couriers.

Crew North was founded by a Sheffield courier who saw that the people who keep the city moving get almost nothing back: no kit, no recognition, no one to call when something goes wrong.

🎽 Free kit

T-shirt, hi-vis and winter jacket, co-branded with our partners. Riders wear it because they are proud of it.

🏆 Rewards

Courier of the Month, monthly challenges and quarterly prize draws, all powered by partners.

🆘 Help & SOS

A live support network for theft, accidents, breakdowns and questions on tax, insurance and rights.

💷 Member deals

Exclusive discounts from partners, redeemed with a unique member number.

🤝 Meet-ups

Monthly events and free maintenance days hosted at partner venues.

💼 Work

Extra shifts and peak-season jobs shared with a pre-verified pool of drivers.

Verified members only

Every member proves they work in delivery (app profile, company badge or route record) before joining. No bots, no random followers. Every person you reach is a real courier. Free to join, always.

Couriers spend their whole working day on the street, in every postcode, at every hour. They are also an audience that is hard to reach with traditional media, and one that buys a lot.

What our members buy regularly

  • Hire & reward, bike and van insurance
  • Bike, e-bike, moped & van repairs and parts
  • Fuel and EV charging
  • Food, coffee and energy drinks on the go
  • Phones, mounts, power banks, data plans
  • Accountancy and Self Assessment help
  • Workwear, waterproofs, gloves and boots

Who they are

  • Parcel couriers (self-employed & multi-drop)
  • Food delivery riders on bikes, e-bikes & mopeds
  • Van drivers and same-day couriers
  • Multi-app workers, often on 2–3 platforms
  • A diverse, multilingual and loyal community

Member profile data (vehicle, area, platforms) is collected at sign-up and shared with partners in aggregated, anonymous form only (UK GDPR compliant).
